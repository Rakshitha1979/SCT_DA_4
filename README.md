Marketing Campaign Analysis 📊
About
This project analyzes marketing campaign data to understand customer behaviour, campaign performance, product spending, and purchasing channels.

Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab
  
Key Analysis
- Campaign response analysis
- Campaign acceptance comparison
- Customer spending by product category
- Purchases by marketing channel
- Web conversion rate
- Correlation heatmap
- ROI-based marketing recommendation

Key Findings
- Total customers: 2,240
- Campaign response rate: 14.91%
- Highest campaign acceptance: Campaign 4 – 167
- Highest purchase channel: Store – 12,970
- Highest spending category: Wines – 680,816

Recommendation
Based on the ROI analysis, the Web channel is recommended for additional marketing investment due to its strong purchase activity and estimated ROI.
